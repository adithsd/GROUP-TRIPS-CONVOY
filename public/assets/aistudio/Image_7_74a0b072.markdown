## Brand & Style
This design system adapts a precision-driven minimalist aesthetic into a sleek, high-contrast dark-mode navigation system tailored for fleet management and real-time logistics. The design evokes absolute reliability, operational clarity, and futuristic control. 

The aesthetic style merges **Glassmorphism** and **Functional Minimalism**: deep slate gray foundations (`#0F172A`) paired with translucent, frosted glass panels that maintain spatial context, combined with vibrant neon accents that instantly communicate vehicle states, speeds, and critical alerts without visual noise.

## Colors
The color palette is optimized for low-light cockpits and long-duration operational monitoring, prioritizing high contrast and instant status recognition.

- **Base Background (`#0F172A`):** Deep slate gray canvas that minimizes eye fatigue and maximizes contrast for data visualizations and maps.
- **Translucent Glass Panels (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.85)`):** Floating navigation overlays and sidebars featuring a heavy backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`) to preserve underlying map and route visibility.
- **Primary Accent (`#06B6D4`):** Electric cyan used strictly for primary interactive controls, active routes, and key selection states.
- **Status Accents:** 
  - **Success / All-Good (`#10B981`):** Emerald green for optimal vehicle health, clear routes, and normal operations.
  - **Warnings / Pit-Stops (`#F59E0B`):** Amber for approaching maintenance, fuel alerts, or minor delays.
  - **Alerts / Errors (`#F43F5E`):** Coral for critical route deviations, mechanical failures, or emergency stops.

## Typography
Typography is engineered for maximum legibility at a glance, utilizing clean grotesque letterforms and strict structural hierarchies.

- **Tabular Numerals:** All speeds, ETAs, coordinates, and telemetry metrics must enforce `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to guarantee vertical alignment without jitter during real-time updates.
- **Scale & Weight:** Headings use tight tracking and semi-bold weights for scannability, while body copy remains neutral and open.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a disciplined fluid grid system adapted for dense telemetry dashboards and navigation overlays.

- **Spacing Rhythm:** Based on an 8px scale, maintaining consistent breathing room across floating panels, map widgets, and sidebar controls.
- **Responsive Behavior:** On desktop viewports, layouts utilize multi-column bento grids for concurrent map viewing and telemetry monitoring. On mobile, panels collapse into single-column stacking drawers with touch-optimized margins.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is achieved through a combination of translucent glassmorphism and subtle ambient lighting rather than heavy drop shadows.

- **Glassmorphism Layers:** Floating cards and navigation headers use `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.85)` surfaces paired with a `20px` background blur to establish clear z-index hierarchy over dynamic map layers.
- **Low-Contrast Outlines:** Panels are defined by ultra-thin, semi-transparent borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`) that catch ambient light without introducing visual clutter.

## Shapes
The shape language favors modern rounded geometry, balancing structural precision with friendly ergonomics.

- **Corner Radii:** Standard interactive elements and cards use a base `0.5rem` to `1rem` radius, while primary buttons and status badges adopt fully rounded pill shapes (`9999px`) to immediately signal interactivity and status.

## Components
Consistent styling rules for core UI elements within the navigation ecosystem:

- **Buttons:** Primary actions utilize electric cyan (`#06B6D4`) fills with pill-shaped borders and high-contrast dark text. Secondary actions use subtle translucent slate backgrounds with light text.
- **Chips & Badges:** Compact status indicators featuring soft background tints paired with solid semantic text colors (emerald for all-good, amber for warnings, coral for alerts).
- **Lists & Telemetry Rows:** Grouped inside translucent glass containers, separated by hair-thin dividers, featuring tabular numerals for real-time speed and ETA readouts.
- **Inputs & Search Fields:** Dark slate input wells with subtle borders that transition to electric cyan focus rings upon interaction.
- **Cards:** Frosted glass panels (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.85)`) with rounded corners, utilized for containing route summaries, vehicle telemetry feeds, and driver notes.
